Rubble Run

You are flying a small drone through a collapsed building, looking for survivors. The building is five floors deep and mostly caved in. You see one floor at a time — the floor you are on — and you carry your own light, so most of it is dark until you go there.

rubble floor shaft down opening up survivor way out

How to find someone. A survivor within range shows as a green dot. A solid pulsing dot is on your floor — fly onto it and it counts automatically. A dashed hollow ring labelled ▲ 1 floor or ▼ 2 floors is not on your floor, and flying over it will do nothing at all until you change floors.

How to change floors — one key. Tap SPACE (or the round blue button on a phone). One tap moves you a whole floor; there is nothing to hold. It only works where there is a hole, so when a survivor is on another floor every cell you could pass through starts pulsing blue — fly onto one and tap. The button only appears when the move is actually available, and the column on the right shows all five floors, which one you are on, and how many people are still waiting on each.

Then get out. Once all three are found, fly back to the dashed blue square you started at before the battery runs down.
