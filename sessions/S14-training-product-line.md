# S14 — Training & screening product line evaluation

**Objective:** Determine whether a training/simulation/screening product for
real pilots and live-ready SAR agencies is a viable near-term route — and if
so, recommend its sequencing against the autonomy path.

**Read:** `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/00-vision.md`, `docs/02-constraints.md` (§C6),
`docs/decisions/ADR-006-training-product-line.md`, `research/V0-summary.md`,
`research/V5-procurement.md`, `research/V6-operator-quals.md`,
`research/R3-platforms.md`
**Owns:** `research/T*`, `docs/decisions/ADR-006*`
**Depends on:** S12 (V5, V6 especially). Can start once V6 lands.
**Budget:** medium-large. Research + strategy; no code.

## Threads

### T1 — Incumbent landscape → `research/T1-incumbents.md`
- What SAR and helicopter training simulators do agencies already own and
  trust? Full-flight sims, part-task trainers, UAS trainers, VR products.
- Who are the vendors, what do they cost, what is the refresh cycle?
- **Answer honestly: is there a gap, or would we be building a worse copy?**
  If incumbents are good, ADR-006 option 3 (feed our scenarios into their
  simulators) is the recommendation, and that is a fine outcome — say it
  plainly rather than manufacturing a reason to build.

### T2 — Domain transfer → `research/T2-transfer.md`
**The weakest link in the proposal; test it first.**
- Does a 4-DOF confined-space small-UAS model transfer to manned rotary-wing
  SAR at all? Different scale, different physics, different risk, different
  cognition. Be skeptical.
- If it does not transfer, which of the four ADR-006 options survive? (Likely
  the UAS-operator ones do and the helicopter ones do not — that is still a
  product, just a narrower one.)
- What *does* transfer across both: search strategy, coverage discipline,
  scene interpretation, crew workload? These may be the real shared substrate.

### T3 — Efficacy evidence bar → `research/T3-efficacy.md`
- What evidence does an agency need to adopt a training tool? Training-transfer
  studies, instructor validation, alignment to a syllabus?
- Compare that bar to the airworthiness bar in V3. Quantify the gap — this is
  the core claim of ADR-006 and it should be evidenced, not asserted.
- Does an accepted training-efficacy methodology exist we should design toward?

### T4 — Scenario-generation-as-a-service → `research/T4-scenario-service.md`
- Can S04's pipeline export to formats existing simulators ingest? Which ones?
- **Mission rehearsal**: how fresh would post-event data need to be to be
  useful, and is that achievable? This is the highest-operational-value option
  and the one with a real technical unknown.
- Integration and partnership routes with incumbent vendors.

### T5 — Expert data flywheel → `research/T5-expert-data.md`
- If certified professionals fly our scenarios, we get **expert-labelled**
  trajectories. Quantify the value: how much expert data is worth how much
  crowd data for S09's training?
- Consent and IP: who owns telemetry generated by an agency's staff on duty?
  Very different regime from consumer players — likely contractual, not
  consent-based. This may be *easier* than the consumer path.
- Does an expert baseline strengthen S09's benchmark? (Almost certainly yes:
  it gives a human-expert reference line, not just an algorithmic one.)
